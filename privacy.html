Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy covers the in-house publishing tool used by Ground Truth Maps and Lighthouse Explains ("we"). The tool posts videos that we make to our own TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside our team can sign in to it.

What the tool accesses

  • Basic profile details of our own accounts (account ID and display name), used to confirm we are posting to the right account.
  • Access tokens that the platforms issue when we sign in. These are stored only on our own computer and are never shared.
  • The videos, captions and post IDs of content we publish ourselves.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process data about other users, viewers or followers.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when we disconnect an account or revoke access in the platform's settings. Post records are kept only to track our own publishing history. To ask about this policy, or to request deletion of any data, email mysticalross4@gmail.com.
